Technician category Common job types What to consider when quoting
Electrical Lighting, outlets, wiring, fault finding Number of fixtures, fault complexity, and parts
Plumbing Leaks, taps, blocked drains, fixture installation Type of repair, access to pipes, and replacement parts
AC & refrigeration Installation, servicing, cooling issues Number of units, diagnosis, and any parts or refrigerant
Carpentry Doors, shelves, cabinets, furniture repairs Measurements, repair complexity, and materials
Painting & finishing Room painting, touch-ups, surface preparation Area to cover, surface condition, and paint requirements

i These are quote factors, not fixed rates. Discuss labor, materials, and any additional work with the customer before beginning.
